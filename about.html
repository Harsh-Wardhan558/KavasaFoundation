About the Event

Kavasa Foundation is a nonprofit dedicated to creating meaningful social impact through innovation, education, and empowerment. The foundation works across key areas including skill development, environmental sustainability, community development, and healthcare support for underprivileged communities.

With a strong commitment to collaboration and change-making, Kavasa Foundation partners with government bodies, corporates, and volunteers to build solutions that uplift lives and strengthen communities.

Cause for Run

Over the years, breakthroughs in cancer screening and treatment have dramatically improved survival rates, giving hope to millions. Yet, many individuals still lose their battle simply because the disease is discovered too late or because advanced treatment remains out of reach for them financially.

Our marathon aims to address this gap by creating a strong platform for awareness and action. The event focuses on educating communities about the importance of regular check-ups, recognizing early symptoms, and supporting timely access to care. It also serves as a fundraiser to assist underprivileged patients who require essential cancer treatment.

Participants can join in any way that suits them — as solo runners, families, corporate groups, or members of the medical community. With accessible categories like 3km, 5 km and 10 km, the marathon welcomes people of all fitness levels to get involved.

Let’s unite with purpose, run to inspire change, and stand together in the ongoing fight against cancer. Your steps can help save lives.
