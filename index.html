Our Mission

Running for a cause that matters. Every step you take helps us support cancer patients and create awareness about early detection and treatment.

Healthcare Support

Providing financial assistance for cancer treatment to underprivileged patients who cannot afford medical care.

Awareness & Education

Spreading awareness about early cancer detection, symptoms, and the importance of regular health check-ups.

Community Impact

Building a stronger, healthier community through fitness, unity, and collective action for social good.

About Kavasa Foundation

We are dedicated to creating sustainable change in underprivileged communities. Through education, healthcare, and empowerment initiatives, we strive to build a better future for all.

The Annual Kavasa Marathon is our flagship fundraising event, bringing together people from all walks of life to celebrate health, unity, and giving back.
